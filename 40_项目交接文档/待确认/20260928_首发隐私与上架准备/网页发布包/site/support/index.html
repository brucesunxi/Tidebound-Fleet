Contact the team

For Tidebound Fleet support, privacy enquiries, or a parent’s request, email tideboundfleet@hotmail.com.

Compose support email

For a game issue, describe what happened and, if available, the game version, device model, and approximate time. Share only what is needed to investigate. Avoid including private information in screenshots.

For purchase issues, include a transaction reference only when needed. We never need your payment password or full card details.

To request access or deletion, see Data requests.

联系游戏团队

游戏问题、隐私咨询及监护人请求均可发送至 tideboundfleet@hotmail.com。

反馈游戏问题时，可说明发生经过，并在方便时提供游戏版本、设备型号和大致时间。只提供排查所需信息，截图请避开个人隐私。

购买问题仅在需要时提供交易参考；不要发送支付密码或完整银行卡号。删除数据请查看数据请求说明。

撰写支持邮件

网页不会自动发送邮件，也没有自动收集反馈的表单。
