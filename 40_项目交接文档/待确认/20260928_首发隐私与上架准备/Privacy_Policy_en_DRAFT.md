## 1. Scope

This policy explains how we process information when providing Tidebound Fleet and related support. The game offers spatial puzzles, ship collections, and cartoon sea-monster battles. The launch plan includes Google AdMob rewarded ads, game analytics operated by us, and Google Play in-app purchases. We plan to distribute through all countries and regions selectable in Google Play, subject to local store availability.

[TO CONFIRM: the release retains play without a registered account.] Core progress, items, and preferences are stored on your device. Analytics does not itself provide cloud saves or cross-device synchronization. We will update this explanation if we introduce accounts, cloud saves, or new processing purposes.

## 2. Information and purposes

The game stores progress, coins, tools, collections, equipped items, settings, and records preventing duplicate game settlements in application storage. These records operate the game and preserve your items and preferences. Local storage alone does not mean that the information is uploaded. Analytics and purchase transmissions are described separately below.

## 5. Children and teenagers

The planned design audience includes players approximately ten years old and older, with distribution planned across all countries and regions selectable in Google Play. [TO CONFIRM: final target age groups and regional implementation rules.] Definitions of a child and requirements for parental consent vary by region; a single worldwide age threshold does not replace applicable requirements.

[The following safeguards are proposed and must be implemented before this section is finalized:]

- Use a neutral process before enabling features that require age differentiation, retaining only the necessary age-category result.
- For children and users of unknown age, do not use interest-based advertising or remarketing or transmit prohibited advertising/device identifiers. Use appropriate ad content, formats, and compliant SDKs.
- Keep optional, individually linkable behavioral analytics disabled by default for children and unknown-age users. Limit other necessary processing to what applicable law permits and obtain verifiable parental consent first where required.
- Allow minors and parents or guardians to contact us about access or deletion. If we discover children’s information collected inappropriately, stop the relevant processing and take appropriate deletion measures.

This draft does not establish that age screening or parental controls already exist in the development build.

## 6. Choices and rights

[TO CONFIRM AND IMPLEMENT: in-game privacy options, advertising consent management, and separate analytics controls.] Where applicable, you can review or change advertising choices and separately change optional analytics choices. Advertising consent does not automatically authorize our analytics. Declining personalized ads does not necessarily eliminate all data processing by advertising services.

Depending on applicable law, you or your parent or guardian may have rights to access, correct or delete information, restrict or object to processing, receive portable data, withdraw consent, or complain to a competent authority.

Send requests to [tideboundfleet@hotmail.com](mailto:tideboundfleet@hotmail.com). A data-deletion information page will also provide this address; its public URL will be provided when the website launches. We request only information necessary to locate relevant records and use appropriate verification. You do not need to create a game account that otherwise does not exist. If we do not retain an identifier that can locate particular records, we will explain what we can address.

You can clear local progress through your device’s application data controls. **Uninstalling or clearing local data does not automatically delete records previously transmitted to our servers, advertising providers, or Google Play.** We process server-side requests within their applicable scope and explain legally required retention. Deleting game data does not delete your Google account or automatically request a refund.
