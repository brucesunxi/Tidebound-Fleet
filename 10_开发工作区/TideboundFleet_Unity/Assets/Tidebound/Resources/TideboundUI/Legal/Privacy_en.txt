Tidebound Fleet · Privacy Policy
Updated: 28 September 2026
Scope: current development build with optional analytics

1. Scope
This notice describes the data used by this build and is always available in Home Settings. No account is required. Advertising, real-money payments, cloud saves and online rankings are not enabled. If these services are introduced, this notice and the relevant choices will be updated first.

2. Local data
The game stores level progress, the current attempt and ship positions, coins, tools, collections, equipment and records that prevent duplicate rewards or settlements on your device. Language, music and sound preferences are also stored locally. These records allow the game to resume and calculate rewards and inventory correctly.

3. Transfers and permissions
This build does not upload complete game saves. Optional gameplay analytics is off by default. Only after you confirm you are 18 or older and opt in, we send random installation and session IDs, app version, platform, level starts/resumes/completions/restarts/deadlocks, tool types and active play time to understand gameplay and returning visits. Under-18s, unknown ages and players who decline do not send this analytics. Names, phone numbers, email, contacts, photos and payment credentials are not collected for analytics. On supported platforms, the initial language selection may use the system language or a country/region code supplied by the device. This is not precise location; you can choose Chinese or English manually.
Information handled independently by your operating system or app store is governed by their own notices. Whether system backups include app data depends on your device settings; this is not a cloud-save service provided by the game.

4. Retention and deletion
Game saves remain in this device's app storage. Consented analytics is sent over HTTPS to Neon. Detailed events are kept for at most 90 days; active installation records support retention measurements. In Settings > Privacy Policy > Data choices, you can disable analytics and request deletion of uploaded records. Offline withdrawal immediately stops collection; deletion completes after reconnection. Uninstalling or clearing local storage does not automatically delete server records; contact the email below first if you need help. You can view game progress and collections and change language or audio settings. Clearing app data or uninstalling can remove your progress. This build offers no developer-hosted recovery service. Any retained operating-system backup is controlled through device and backup settings.

5. Sharing and security
We do not sell game saves or analytics. Vercel hosts the analytics API and Neon provides database storage; their infrastructure handles the network information needed for requests. The API uses a short-lived hashed network identifier for registration rate limits and does not store raw IP addresses in game analytics tables. Gameplay analytics is used to improve this game, not for advertising profiles. App storage and duplicate-settlement checks help protect progress. Device damage or data deletion can still cause loss. Install only from trusted sources.

6. Younger players
Children should use the game and read this notice with a parent or guardian. Registration, chat, real-money purchases and active advertising are not present in this build. Optional analytics is available only to players who explicitly confirm they are 18 or older and opt in. Full dates of birth are not stored.

7. Choices and contact
You can change language, turn music or sounds off, stop playing or manage local data. Audio and language choices do not change earned rewards. The operator is Beijing Jianrong Yishu Technology Co., Ltd. (北京简融易数科技有限公司). For analytics access, deletion or support, contact tideboundfleet@hotmail.com. Do not send account passwords or payment secrets.

8. Publication details
This notice covers optional analytics in the current development build. Any future advertising or real-money payment features will be accompanied by updated information and choices. Nothing here limits your rights under applicable law. This notice will be updated when data practices change.
