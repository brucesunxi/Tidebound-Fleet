Tidebound Fleet · Privacy Policy
Updated: 26 September 2026
Scope: current offline development build

1. Scope
This notice describes the data used by this build and is always available in Home Settings. No account is required. Advertising, real-money payments, cloud saves and online rankings are not enabled. If these services are introduced, this notice and the relevant choices will be updated first.

2. Local data
The game stores level progress, the current attempt and ship positions, coins, tools, collections, equipment and records that prevent duplicate rewards or settlements on your device. Language, music and sound preferences are also stored locally. These records allow the game to resume and calculate rewards and inventory correctly.

3. Transfers and permissions
This build has no feature that uploads these saves to a developer server and no advertising or analytics SDK reporting. It does not require your name, phone number, email address, contacts or photos. On supported platforms, the initial language selection may use the system language or a country/region code supplied by the device. This is not precise location; you can choose Chinese or English manually.
Information handled independently by your operating system or app store is governed by their own notices. Whether system backups include app data depends on your device settings; this is not a cloud-save service provided by the game.

4. Retention and deletion
Data remains in this device's app storage. You can view game progress and collections and change language or audio settings. Clearing app data or uninstalling can remove your progress. This build offers no developer-hosted recovery service. Any retained operating-system backup is controlled through device and backup settings.

5. Sharing and security
This build does not sell or externally share game saves, or transmit them to advertising companies. App storage and duplicate-settlement checks help protect progress. Device damage or data deletion can still cause loss. Install only from trusted sources.

6. Younger players
Children should use the game and read this notice with a parent or guardian. Registration, chat, real-money purchases and active advertising are not present in this build.

7. Choices and contact
You can change language, turn music or sounds off, stop playing or manage local data. Audio and language choices do not change earned rewards. If installed from an app store, use the developer contact listed on that store page for questions.

8. Publication details
This notice accompanies an offline development build. The operator identity, working contact channel and public policy URL must be completed before store release; they are not finalized in this build. Nothing here limits your rights under applicable law. This notice will be updated when data practices change.
