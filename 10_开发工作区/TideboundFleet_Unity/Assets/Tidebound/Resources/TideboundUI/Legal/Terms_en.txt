Tidebound Fleet · Terms of Use
Updated: 26 September 2026
Scope: current offline development build

1. The game
Tidebound Fleet is a ship movement puzzle game. Ships leave the puzzle in their permitted direction, follow the outer lane and enter the battle area under the current game rules. This build works offline without account registration. Obtain it from trusted sources and comply with applicable law.

2. Progress and items
Progress, coins, tools, collections and equipment are saved on your device. Game coins and items serve game functions, have no cash value and cannot currently be redeemed, transferred or traded between players. Rewards and inventory follow the displayed rules and actual game records.

3. Pause and navigation
Pausing stops the current puzzle, lane and battle flow. Continue returns to that attempt. Restart immediately restarts the current level without a second confirmation; settlements and consumed tools follow the existing rules. Exit game returns to the harbour home screen. Start Game on the home screen opens the next uncleared level from its initial state. It does not resume ship positions, lane travel, combat or combos from an unfinished or failed attempt. Credited coins, clear stars, collections and remaining tool inventory are kept; consumed tools are not refunded. The existing restart settlement rules apply to unfinished attempts.

4. Ads and purchases
This build has no live advertising or real-money payment service. Skip level is disabled and does not show ads, charge money or advance progress. Existing offline coin purchases follow their displayed in-game rules. Any future advertising, payments or online services will have their relevant terms and choices presented separately.

5. Local data
Please read the Privacy Policy for storage details. Clearing app data, device failure or uninstalling may remove progress. Developer-hosted recovery is not currently available. Game items should not be treated as redeemable property.
